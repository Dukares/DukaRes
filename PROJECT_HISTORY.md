# Project History

## The Beginning

The idea for DukaRes did not start as a software project.

It started as a question.

After many years working in hospitality, tourism, booking systems, and property management, I constantly saw the same challenges faced by property owners.

Many businesses depended heavily on third-party platforms, complex software, and systems that did not always match their real needs.

## The Search For A Better Solution

For years I worked with existing hospitality software and followed the evolution of the industry.

During that journey I learned what worked, what did not work, and what property owners were really looking for.

Over time a new idea started to take shape.

Not a small improvement.

A completely new project.

## The Birth Of DukaRes

DukaRes was created with a long-term vision.

The objective was simple:

Create a modern hospitality platform built around real business needs.

The project started from a blank page and continues to evolve step by step.

## The Development Journey

DukaRes follows a structured roadmap.

Every phase is developed, tested, improved, and stabilized before moving forward.

The focus is always on quality, stability, and long-term sustainability.

## Today

DukaRes remains under active development.

New features, improvements, and innovations continue to be added through every completed phase of the roadmap.

## The Future

The journey is still at the beginning.

The long-term goal is to create a modern platform that helps property owners manage their business with greater control, flexibility, and independence.

Every completed step brings that vision closer to reality.
