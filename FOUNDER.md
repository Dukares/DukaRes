# Founder

## Bujar Duka

Founder of DukaRes.

DukaRes was created from a simple idea:

Property owners deserve more control over their business, their bookings, and their future.

For many years I have been involved in the hospitality industry, working directly with hotels, property owners, tourism businesses, and booking platforms.

This experience provided a deep understanding of the challenges faced by accommodation providers every day.

After years of using existing hospitality software, I decided to start building a new platform from the ground up.

That platform became DukaRes.

## The Vision

DukaRes is being developed as a modern hospitality platform focused on:

* Direct Bookings
* Property Management
* Real Availability
* Owner Independence
* Scalability
* Innovation

The project is built step by step with a long-term vision and a strong commitment to quality.

## Why DukaRes

The hospitality industry continues to evolve.

Property owners need modern tools that give them flexibility, transparency, and control.

DukaRes was created to help meet those needs.

## Philosophy

Build slowly.

Build correctly.

Build for the future.

## Current Status

DukaRes is under active development and continues to grow with every completed phase of the roadmap.

The journey has only just begun.
